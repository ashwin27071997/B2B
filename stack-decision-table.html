Deadline: 18 Aug 2026

Oracle cut Always Free ARM from 4 OCPU / 24 GB down to 2 OCPU / 12 GB. Free-tier instances above the new limit get terminated after that date.

Block storage was not cut — 200 GB stands. Resize the shape now if you have anything running.

The commercial-use trap

Vercel's Hobby plan forbids commercial sites. Fine for a portfolio, a licensing problem for a client storefront. Cloudflare Pages has no equivalent clause — that is the main reason it wins the frontend row.

Three environments, two projects

Supabase free allows 2 active projects; dev / QA / prod needs 3. Run dev on the local Supabase CLI in Docker and host only QA and prod.

Free projects also pause after 7 days idle — point an UptimeRobot monitor at the QA project's health endpoint to keep it warm between test cycles.

What you cannot get for free

Domain (~₹900/yr), SMS OTP (~₹0.15–0.25 per message), and Razorpay's 2% per transaction. Everything else in this table has a genuine zero-cost path. Budget roughly ₹1,000 up front and ₹150–300/month.
